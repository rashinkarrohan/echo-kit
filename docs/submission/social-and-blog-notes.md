# Blog and social notes (write these yourself)
The Social Blitz is judged on original thinking, not AI writing, and the profile must match your Devpost details. Draft in your own words.

Social post prompts: your favourite thing about Kiro; the one moment it changed how you work (a hook, a spec, a property test that caught a bug); what surprised you. Start the post with #KiroweenHackathon26.

Blog outline: why older adults with hearing loss; how the anchor check works; what Kiro did well and badly; what you would change. Say near the top: "This post is written as part of the Kiroween 2026 hackathon." Publish on a third-party platform before the submission period ends.
