# Devpost description (draft; keep every claim consistent with docs/CLAIMS.md)

**Echo Kit** is a skeleton for apps that turn a consented spoken conversation into a receipt you can check. Every extracted fact links to the audio where it was said, and anything uncertain is flagged instead of guessed.

**Echo Care** helps older adults with hearing loss and their families during and after a doctor visit or at the pharmacy counter: large-text captions that flag doses, drug names, frequencies and dates; a one-tap "please repeat" card; a read-back the speaker can see; a receipt with audio-linked facts; an optional label check; and a family share the patient controls.

**Echo Cover** helps the same people before a purchase: a sourced question card, a side-by-side comparison of what different sellers said, a Not discussed flag for anything skipped, and a "please confirm in writing" draft.

**Built with Kiro:** specs and property-based tests, steering, hooks, a custom MCP server, a published power, custom agents and a separate Crew app. See docs/KIRO.md.

**Honest limits:** prototype; not an interpreter or a medical, legal or financial adviser; tested with actors and a small number of volunteers (see docs/EVIDENCE.md); studies suggest poor communication with hearing-impaired patients contributes to medication errors, and we make no claim that Echo reduces errors.

**Platform:** web app for phone browsers. **Try it:** see the README "Try it in 3 clicks".
