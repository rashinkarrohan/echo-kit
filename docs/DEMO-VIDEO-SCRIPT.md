# Demo video script (under 3 minutes)

The rules: under 3 minutes, public on YouTube, **shown on the phone the app is built for**, no third-party music or logos, English. Judges are not required to watch past 3:00, so the strongest moment goes in the first two minutes.

The idea to land: **Echo Kit never guesses. Every fact quotes the words it came from, and when it is unsure it says so.** Show that once, clearly, rather than listing features.

## Before recording

- Use a real phone, Wi-Fi on, volume up, Do Not Disturb on. Screen-record with the phone's own recorder, with its microphone off so the actor audio comes from the app.
- Use only the fictional sample consults (actors with releases, `docs/RELEASES.md`). No real patient or seller data on screen.
- Add a row to `docs/RELEASES.md` for anyone who appears or speaks in the video, including you.
- No music, or music you made or have a licence for. Add English captions.
- Open both apps once first so they are cached and load fast.

## Moments to get right

1. **The misheard sample is the proof.** Give it a full 30 seconds and let the "Check this" reason be readable on screen.
2. **Say what is tested and what is not.** The 83 of 83 is scripted conversations. If you mention real speech, say 12 real GP consultations, and that Echo marked every unsure medicine Check this (`docs/SPIKES.md`).
3. **Keep your own voice.** Short, plain sentences. Do not read the table above word for word.

## After recording

- Upload to YouTube as **Public**. Paste the link into the README ("Demo video") and the Devpost form.
- Watch it once on the phone with the sound off (captions on) and once with sound.
- Check the length is under 3:00 on the YouTube page.
