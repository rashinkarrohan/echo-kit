# Appendix C: screens, copy deck and states

Kiro builds each screen exactly as written. Where copy is given it is final; Kiro does not invent advice or claims. Both apps: English, phone portrait first, large text (see DESIGN-TOKENS.md), every screen has loading, empty, error and permission-denied states, and every list of results has an empty state.

## Global copy
- Consent wording (all-party everywhere): "Echo will record this conversation on your phone. Everyone in the conversation must agree. Audio and text stay on your phone unless you choose to share. You can stop and delete at any time."
- Disclaimer (Care): "Echo records and summarises what was said. It is not medical advice and not an interpreter."
- Disclaimer (Cover): "Echo compares what sellers said. It is not legal or financial advice and it does not recommend a purchase."
- Uncertainty line on captions: "Captions can be wrong. Numbers and medicine names are highlighted so you can check them."
- Mic denied: "Echo can't hear anything yet. Allow the microphone in your browser settings, then try again."
- Model loading: "Getting ready. This happens once and then works without internet."
- Generic error: "Something went wrong. Nothing was sent anywhere. Try again."

## Echo Care screens
Echo Care follows the Echo Care design pack (mobile 390 px, desktop 1280 px, switch at 960 px). The pack's colours, type and components are in DESIGN-TOKENS.md and `apps/echo-care/src/design/`. Mobile has a floating bottom bar (Home, Visits, Medicines) and a Text size button on Home; desktop has a header with the logo, the same three links and the Text size button. Where the pack names who spoke ("Doctor") the app says "Heard", because Echo cannot tell speakers apart. Where the pack says "To check" the app says "Check this", the product's stamp word.
1. `/home`: title "Read what your doctor says, as they say it." Tag "Doctor and pharmacy visits". Primary "Start a visit", secondary "Try a practice visit" (the scripted actor consult, no microphone). A "Last visit, today" card opens that visit's summary. Links: "Start at the pharmacy counter", "How Echo did in tests". A privacy line and the disclaimer. No microphone or storage access is needed to view this screen. "Visit deleted from this phone." shows once after a delete.
2. `/doctor-card` (Step 1 of 2): "Hand your phone to the doctor." A teal card: "I am deaf or hard of hearing." "Please face me and speak slowly, one short sentence at a time. May I use my phone to write down what you say? Sound is not saved." (desktop says "screen"). Buttons "They said yes. Start" and "They said no. Go back". A switch "Ask for a sign language interpreter next time" (kept on this device; it adds a line to the Ask card).
3. `/consent` (Step 2 of 2, shown as the microphone step): "Echo Care needs your microphone". "Sound is never saved", "Only listens while captions are on". The all-party consent wording, then one switch per purpose: "Record this conversation" and "Turn speech into text on this device". "Allow microphone" stays off until both are on, then grants both in the consent ledger and starts. "Not now" goes home. Nothing is on by default.
4. `/record`: live captions. Newest line large with the Heard label, earlier lines smaller above. Flagged words are yellow with an underline; a medicine name that needs checking gets a short hint ("Sounds like metformin?") and a live card with "Check this". Status pill (Listening, Paused, Getting ready). Text size A A A (phone: 28, 34, 40 px; desktop: 44, 52, 60 px). Controls: "Please repeat", Pause or Resume, "Finish visit", "Discard recording" (asks first). "Yellow words are worth checking. Captions can be wrong." is always shown. While audio may be captured no link leads away. A refused or missing microphone shows "Try again" and "Back to home".
5. `/repeat`: dark screen, "What did you miss?" with four tiles (Medicine name, Amount, How often, All of it); the yellow card shows "Please say the medicine name again." and so on. It starts on the tile that matches the live check. "I got it. Back to captions".
6. `/receipt` (Visit summary): "Your visit summary" (desktop "What was said"), the date, "At a glance" (Heard clearly, Check this, Not said), a card per medicine (name, Amount, How often, For how long, "Not said" for anything missing, the words that were said, Hear it and Slower, and a plain-words note when it needs checking), "Next visit" (timing as heard, "Exact date: not said" unless a date was said, and "Ask the front desk for a date"), and "Questions & safety". A visit restored after reload says "Audio not kept". "Show the doctor this" opens the read-back.
7. `/questions`: "When to call the clinic" shows the words that were said, never invented ones; "My questions" lists the planned questions as Answered, Unclear or Not discussed, with "Ask about <topic>" for each one not answered. Then "At the pharmacy: Check the label matches", "Share with family", and "Delete this visit" (asks first, says it cannot be undone).
8. `/ask`: dark screen with the user's own question (or the request for a date) in a large yellow card to hold up to the person at the desk.
9. `/read-back`: for each medicine fact: "I heard: metformin, 500 milligrams, twice daily." "Right" and "Not right". A "Show the doctor" toggle flips to a speaker-facing view. Items are confirmed only by a tap.
10. `/pharmacy`: the same card and steps for the pharmacist ("Message for the pharmacist"), then the pharmacy-counter schema.
11. `/label`: "Take a photo of the label" (or "Type it in"); three fields to confirm. Result: "Matches what was said" or a flag card with both anchors. No verdict on who is right. A link to record the pharmacy conversation.
12. `/share`: first a consent switch "Share a summary with family" (off until chosen), then choose visits, sections and recipient; "Share (no audio)" uses the share sheet; warning: "A file you send can't be taken back."
13. `/visits`: "Your visits": saved visits on this device, newest first, each with a date, what was heard and "Open". Empty, loading and storage-error states.
14. `/medicines`: "Your medicines": names only (no doses; up to 20), saved on this device. Added names are capitalised. Remove shows an "Undo" bar. Echo listens for these names first; a name it is unsure of is still marked Check this. States: loading, empty, list, input error, save failed.
15. `/trust`: eval numbers: facts kept, dropped, invented (target 0), unanchored (target 0), flagged-token recall, runs, date.
    Every page has one h1 that takes focus on arrival, and every state (loading, empty, error, permission denied) has a message with a way forward.
## Echo Cover screens
Echo Cover follows the Echo Cover design pack (mobile 390 px, desktop 1280 px, switch at 960 px): blue palette, the same type and shape rules as Care, Phosphor icons, and a logo of a shield with five sound bars. The pack's colours are in DESIGN-TOKENS.md; the code is in `apps/echo-cover/src/design/`. Mobile has a floating bottom bar (Home, Compare, Test results) on Home, Seller comparison and Test results, and a Text size button on Home and Test results; desktop has a header with the logo, the same three links and the Text size button. Chips always carry an icon and a word.
1. `/home`: blue hero, title "Compare what sellers said, side by side." Tag "For hearing-aid purchases". Primary "Start comparing", secondary "Play sample consult" with the note that the sample uses actors, made-up prices, no microphone, nothing kept. A "Seller comparison" example (Said / Not discussed per seller), "How Echo Cover works" in three steps, "Private by default" and the Cover disclaimer. When saved live conversations exist, a card shows their count and opens "Open saved comparisons". The picture of two offers uses made-up numbers and a mark between them that does not pick one.
2. `/saved`: "Saved seller conversations": newest first, with seller name, date, what was heard and "Open seller receipt". With two or more, "Compare these sellers". Empty, loading and storage-error states.
3. `/consent` (Step 1 of 3, Your choices): "Before we start", the all-party wording, then one On/Off switch per purpose: "Record this conversation" and "Turn speech into text on my phone". "I agree and continue" stays off until both are on. There is no share switch because Echo Cover has no share action. The pack's line "Echo keeps a recording on your phone only" is worded "Echo records on your phone only. Sound is not saved after you close the page." because sound is never stored. The storage fallback is shown when receipts cannot be kept.
4. `/questions` (Step 2 of 3, Plan questions): "Questions to ask before you buy"; "Turn on the ones you plan to ask." and "N of 5 chosen". Each question is a card with "See source" and an On/Off switch named after the question; a chosen card has a blue border. Button "Start recording a seller".
5. `/record` (Step 3 of 3, Seller N): first "Who are you talking to?" with the required, unique "Seller or clinic name" and "Start recording". Then live captions: "Recording <seller>" (or "Paused: <seller>") with a dot, one card per caption with a blue edge, money, percentages, time periods and commission highlighted in yellow, "Yellow words are money, percentages, time periods and commission. Check them. Captions can be wrong.", "Stop and review", Pause or Resume, and "Discard recording" (asks first). While audio may be captured no link leaves the page. A refused microphone or a model error shows "Try again". The Text size button sets the caption size.
6. `/review/:seller`: "<seller>: what was said". An amber card says "1 of at least 2 sellers recorded. Record one more to compare." (blue once there are two). "Your questions": each question as Said, Check this or Not discussed with Hear it, and "I plan to ask this" for the ones chosen; "What the labels mean"; then one card per fact (Price, What is included, Trial and returns, Financing, Commission) with Heard clearly or Check this, each field (anything not said shows "Not said"), the words that were said, Hear it and Slower (or "Audio not kept"). Actions: "Record another seller", "Compare sellers" (disabled with a reason until two), "Draft a message to confirm in writing", "Delete this seller conversation" (asks first).
7. `/compare`: "Seller comparison", "N sellers recorded", "What each seller said, side by side. Echo does not rank sellers." One card per topic (a row on desktop), sellers in recording order. Each cell shows what the seller said or a dash, Said or Not said, and Hear it (or "Audio not kept"). One note per topic: "The amounts differ", "The terms differ", "The details differ", "Only <names> said this", "Neither seller said" or "The same on each". No ranking, no "best", no highlighting of one seller. Links to each seller's receipt, "Draft a message to confirm in writing" and "Record another seller". With fewer than two sellers: "Record at least two sellers to compare them side by side."
8. `/confirm/:seller`: "Confirm it in writing". The drafted message: "Hello, thank you for your time. To confirm what we discussed: [label: value]. [For each Not discussed question: I would also like to know: question]. Please reply in writing to confirm." Buttons "Copy message" and "Share by email or message" (the device's own share sheet or mail app). "Echo does not send anything. Keep the reply." A row of sellers switches whose message is shown.
9. `/trust`: "How Echo did in tests": facts kept, missed, made up (target 0), no quote (target 0), numbers and names flagged, questions marked correctly, runs and last run, and the note that these are scripted conversations, not real purchases.
Every page has one h1 that takes focus on arrival.
## Sample consult mode (both apps)
A prominent "Play sample consult" button on the home screen runs a bundled scripted consult with its audio and pre-made segments: captions appear on schedule, the receipt (or comparison) builds, anchor chips play the bundled audio. No model download, no microphone, works offline after first load. The audio is an actor reading docs/RECORDING-SCRIPT.md word for word, split at the pauses into the consult's segments (`apps/*/public/samples/*.mp3`). A sample with no recording (the misheard-names test sample) shows "Audio not kept" instead of audio buttons.

## Definition of done per app
- Every screen above exists with its states; axe reports zero serious or critical issues; keyboard-only completes the main flow; 200% zoom has no horizontal scroll; reduced motion removes animation.
- The main flow passes a Playwright test with the sample consult.
- No screen contains advice, a diagnosis, a dose calculation, or a seller ranking.
- Care and Cover are visibly distinct (see the distinctness rule).
