# Echo Kit product rules
Echo turns a consented conversation into a checkable receipt, built first for older adults with unaided hearing loss and their adult children. Two distinct apps on one skeleton: Echo Care (doctor and pharmacy) and Echo Cover (hearing-aid purchase conversations). Every fact needs a verbatim quote and a time range; no anchor, no claim. Captions are never presented as certain: flag detector matches and offer "Please repeat" and read-back. Echo Cover compares quotes and never ranks sellers or recommends a purchase. Echo never gives medical, legal or financial advice and never calculates doses. Say "said", "sounds like", "check this". Audio stays on the device by default. See docs/SPEC-SEED.md and the PRD.
