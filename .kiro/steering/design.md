# Design rules
Receipt metaphor, three stamps (Heard clearly, Check this, Not said), one signature motion (receipt prints line by line). Large text, AA contrast or better, read-aloud in Hindi and English, design every state (consent, recording, processing, low-confidence, empty, error, permission denied). Two skins on shared tokens.
